## 1. HARDWARE ELECTRICAL VERIFICATION

### Voltage Divider & Signal Reconstruction
Both the pH and Turbidity analog modules are powered from $+5.0\,\text{V}$ VCC. To safely scale their $0.0\,\text{V}–5.0\,\text{V}$ outputs for the ESP32 ADC ($3.3\,\text{V}$ maximum safe input), a hardware voltage divider is connected:
$$\text{Divider Ratio} = \frac{22\,\text{k}\Omega}{33\,\text{k}\Omega + 22\,\text{k}\Omega} = 0.400$$
$$\text{Reconstruction Factor} = \frac{1}{0.400} = 2.500$$
$$V_{\text{ADC}} = \text{rawADC} \times \left(\frac{3.300\,\text{V}}{4095}\right), \quad V_{\text{module}} = V_{\text{ADC}} \times 2.500$$
